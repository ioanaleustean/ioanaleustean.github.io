## Short Bio

I am a Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Bucharest[cite: 1, 2]. Between February 2020 and February 2024, I served as the Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science. 

My research spans non-classical logics, algebraic logic, formal verification, and many-sorted modal logics. 


---

## Research Interests

- **Specification and Verification:** Logic for program verification, many-sorted modal logic, matching logic.
- **Many-Valued Logics:** Algebraic and probabilistic aspects, Łukasiewicz logic, MV-algebras, MV-modules, Riesz MV-algebras.
- **Applications:** Logical foundations for neural networks and security protocols.
